## Headlines (hook-writer: 5 to 10 candidates, scored, best kept)
Scoring: gap, specificity, truth (compliance), fits a one-glance read, brand voice. Out of 10.

**14 States Map**

**Umbrella**

**Work Vehicle (commercial auto)**

**Renewal Lock**
